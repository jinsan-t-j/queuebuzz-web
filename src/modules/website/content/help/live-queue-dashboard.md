## The live queue

Every customer who joins shows up on your dashboard in order. From each entry you can call them up, mark them served, or remove a no-show — all with one tap.

## Strict mode

By default, queues run in **strict mode**: only one guest can be called at a time, and you can't call the next person until the current one is marked served. This keeps the line moving in order. You can turn it off from queue settings if you'd rather call several guests at once (e.g. for a large seating area), but this can let guests fall out of order.

## Adding a guest manually

Walk-in without a phone, or taking a booking over the phone? Use **Add Guest** on the dashboard to add someone to the queue directly, without them needing to join themselves.

## Session notes

Add a quick note to any queue entry — table preference, allergy info, anything your staff should see when that guest is called.

## Quick setup

New queues start with sensible defaults. Use **Quick Setup** on the dashboard to adjust capacity, estimated wait time, and other settings without leaving the live view.
