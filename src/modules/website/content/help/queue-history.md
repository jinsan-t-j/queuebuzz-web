## Viewing past sessions

The **History** tab lists every past queue session, with stats like total guests served, average wait time, and no-shows. Click into any session for a full timeline of every guest who passed through it.

## Filtering

Use the filter dropdown to narrow the list by date range or status, useful when you're comparing a specific day or event against your usual traffic.

## Clearing history

If you need to clear old session data, you can do this from **Settings → Danger Zone**. This permanently deletes past queue history and can't be undone.
