## Business profile

Update your business name, address, and cover photo from **Settings**. Your address is also what customer location verification checks against, so keep it accurate.

## Danger zone

**Settings → Danger Zone** has two destructive actions, both requiring confirmation:

- **Clear Queue History** — permanently deletes your past session data.
- **Delete Account** — permanently deletes your QueueBuzz account and all associated data.

Neither of these can be undone, so use them carefully.
