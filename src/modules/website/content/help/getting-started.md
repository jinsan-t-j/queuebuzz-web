## Create your queue page

Sign up and QueueBuzz gives every business a custom queue page at `queuebuzz.app/your-business`. Share that link (or QR code) with walk-in customers so they can join the line from their phone — no app download needed.

## Add your business details

From **Settings → Business Profile**, add your business name, address, and a cover photo. This is what customers see before joining your queue.

## Start seating customers

Once a customer joins, they show up in your live queue dashboard. Call them up, mark them seated, or remove a no-show with one tap. Customers get an SMS/notification update automatically as their position changes.
