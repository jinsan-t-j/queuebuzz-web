description: Plan limits, upgrading to Premium, and managing your subscription.
---

## Free plan limits

The free plan caps things like queue duration, guests per queue, and number of active queues. When you're close to a limit, you'll see a banner on your dashboard letting you know.

## Upgrading to Premium

Premium removes these caps — unlimited queue duration, guests, and queues. You can upgrade any time from the upgrade prompt on your dashboard or from **Settings → Subscription**.

## Managing your subscription

Your current plan, renewal date, and billing details live under **Settings → Subscription**. From there you can also cancel — your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, after which you're moved back to the free plan.
