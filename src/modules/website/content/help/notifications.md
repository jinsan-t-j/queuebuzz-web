## How notifications work

QueueBuzz notifies customers at two points: when they join the queue, and when they're close to being called. No account or app install is required on the customer's side.

## Turning notifications off

If you'd rather manage the queue verbally in-store, you can disable customer notifications entirely from the same settings page. Customers will still see their live position on the queue page.

## iPhone & iPad limitations

Apple's Safari only allows push alerts (sound & vibration) for sites added to the Home Screen — it can't send them to a regular browser tab. On iOS, customers who want the "Buzz" alert need to:

1. Tap Safari's **Share** button and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
2. Launch QueueBuzz from that Home Screen icon (not Safari) to activate alerts.

Without this step, iOS customers can still see their live position updating on the page — they just won't get a sound or vibration when they're called.
